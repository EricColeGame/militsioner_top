## Why the Numbers Keep Changing

There are a few plausible explanations for the conflicting Militsioner cop height figures, and none of them require a conspiracy.

- **Build-to-build tuning.** Developers routinely scale oversized characters up or down to fix camera readability, collision, and performance.
- **Lore versus model.** A wiki may record the character's canonical size from narrative material, while the in-game model is smaller for playability.
- **Marketing shorthand.** A round "30 meters" reads better in a social post than a precise figure.
- **Fan extrapolation.** Community editors sometimes estimate scale from screenshots, which introduces large errors at this magnitude.

If you want the most reliable answer, trust footage over wikis. Compare the cop's height to a known object in the same shot — a streetlamp, a house, a church tower — and do the math yourself. That method will always beat a number copied from a page nobody has verified.
