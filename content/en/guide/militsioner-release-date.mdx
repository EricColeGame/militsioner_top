## What Kind of Game Is Militsioner?

Militsioner is a kafkaesque immersive sim about a nobody who gets arrested for a crime they can't remember committing. You wake up in a surreal town, grab your passport and a half-eaten banana, and start planning an escape — while a Colossal Policeman looms over the rooftops, tracking your behavior.

The Steam tags tell the story better than any marketing copy could:

| Tag | What It Means in Practice |
| --- | --- |
| Immersive Sim | Multiple valid solutions to every problem |
| Stealth | Staying quiet often beats staying fast |
| First-Person | You experience the surveillance up close |
| Nonlinear | No fixed order to your objectives |
| Sandbox / Open World | The town is a persistent, reactive space |
| Dystopian / Surreal | Bureaucratic horror with a wink |
| Replay Value | Failed escapes feed into the next attempt |
| Story Rich | NPC moods and dialogue shift with your actions |

Two design pillars stand out. The first is mood management: you can anger, flatter, disappoint, deceive, or even romance the Colossal Policeman, and he has apparently been "quite moody of late." The second is consequence memory. If you conspire with the train driver and the plan collapses, the train ends up wrecked and the driver injured. Take a job at the station instead, and he walks away fine. The town remembers, and the Policeman adjusts his behavior based on what you tried last time.

That combination — social manipulation layered on top of stealth routes — is why the Militsioner release date keeps appearing on watchlists alongside other high-concept indies.
