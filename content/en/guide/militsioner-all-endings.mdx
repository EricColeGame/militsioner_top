## The Three Train Endings, Step by Step

Every train ending shares one goal — get on the train and leave — but they differ in how much noise you make along the way. Think of them as three answers to the same question: how do you get to the station without the officer deciding you are a problem?

### In & Out

This is the most direct route. Community reports describe it as the ending that rewards confidence over caution: you move toward the station with purpose, keep your conversations short, and board before the officer has a reason to stop you. It is a good first ending to chase because it teaches you how the suspicion system reacts to bold movement.

### Quiet as a Mouse

The stealth-flavored variant. Instead of rushing the station, you spend your time lowering your profile — avoiding confrontations, staying out of sightlines, and letting the officer's attention drift elsewhere naturally. It takes longer, but it is far more forgiving if your timing is imperfect.

### Playing Cupid

The most unusual of the three. Rather than distracting the officer yourself, you meddle in the relationships of the people around you, so his attention lands on their drama instead of your escape. Community reports frame this as the most story-driven train outcome, and it pairs well with the game's social manipulation systems.

| Train ending | What triggers it | Main risk | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| In & Out | Boarding quickly during a calm window | Raising suspicion too early | First-time players |
| Quiet as a Mouse | Reaching departure with low suspicion | Running out of time | Patient, stealth-minded players |

| Romance milestone | What to do | Why it matters |
