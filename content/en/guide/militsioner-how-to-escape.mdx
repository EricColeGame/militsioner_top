## Reading the Colossal Policeman: Moods Are Your Real Map

Most stealth games ask you to avoid a patrol. Militsioner asks you to manage a relationship. The Colossal Policeman is described as someone you can anger, flatter, disappoint, deceive — or even romance. That emotional range is the actual escape system.

| Approach | What It Looks Like in Play | When It Tends to Help |
|---|---|---|
| Flattery | Compliments, gifts, playing along | He is neutral or curious about you |
| Deception | False trails, misdirection, quiet lies | You need time to move or loot |
| Anger | Defiance, confrontation, refusal | Rarely — high risk of tightening security |
| Disappointment | Breaking trust, failed promises | Unintended; recover quickly before he locks in |
| Romance | Flowers, gifts, going on a date | A long, slow play with a big payoff |

Community reports from the demo suggest that his state carries between attempts. In other words, how you treated him last time shapes how closely he watches you now. If you burned him with a lie, expect less slack. If you charmed him, expect a little breathing room — and use it immediately.

## Consequences: The Town Keeps Receipts

One of the clearest examples in the official material involves the train. If you conspire with the train driver and the plan falls apart, the train is wrecked and the driver is injured. Try again with a different approach — say, working at the train station instead — and the driver comes out fine.

That is the design promise in miniature:

- Your actions persist as world state, not just as a fail counter.
- Characters other than the Policeman can be harmed by your plans.
- There is often a gentler, slower path that protects them.
- The Colossal Policeman recalls what you did and adapts his behavior.

| What You Did Last Time | What the World Remembers | How to Respond Now |
|---|---|---|
| Ran loudly through the open street | High visibility, tighter watch | Switch to interiors and vertical routes |
| Lied to the Policeman | Reduced trust, less slack | Repair with gifts or honesty before acting |
| Injured an NPC during a plan | Damaged relationship and world state | Take the slow, legitimate route instead |
| Charmed him successfully | A little breathing room | Use the window immediately, do not waste it |
