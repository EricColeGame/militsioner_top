## What Is Militsioner? A Primer Before You Play

Militsioner is a first-person "runaway simulator" set in a small, oppressive town. You are under arrest for an unspecified crime, and your jailer is not a normal officer — he is a towering Giant Policeman whose reach extends across streets, rooftops, and windows. The tone is deliberately Kafkaesque: bureaucratic, surreal, and quietly threatening.

What separates it from most stealth games is conversation. You can speak to the Giant Policeman and other characters using voice input, which means talking your way out of a bad situation matters as much as sneaking past one. If you go in expecting a shooter or a pure hide-and-seek game, you will misread almost every encounter. Community reports consistently describe the experience as tense, strange, and driven by improvisation rather than reflexes.

## Militsioner Beginner Guide: The Core Systems That Decide Your Escape

New players usually fail because they treat Militsioner like a conventional stealth game. It is not. Four systems do most of the work, and understanding them early saves hours of frustration.

**Line of sight.** The Giant Policeman's gaze is the central threat. Staying behind cover is not enough on its own — you also need to think about elevation, open ground, and how visible you are from a distance. Move between buildings, use walls and vehicles, and avoid long straight streets.

**Attention and suspicion.** Being spotted is rarely an instant loss. What matters is how much attention you accumulate and whether you give the officer a reason to close in. Loud actions, repeated mistakes, and lingering in the open all push things in the wrong direction.

**Voice and dialogue.** Speech is a tool, not decoration. Talking to characters can defuse tension, gather information, or buy you time — but careless lines can also raise suspicion.

**Time and opportunity.** Windows of opportunity open and close. Waiting for the right moment is often smarter than forcing an escape the instant you see a gap.

| System | What It Does | Beginner Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Line of sight | Determines whether you are being watched | Highest |
| Attention level | Tracks how much heat you have drawn | High |
| Voice dialogue | Opens information and distraction options | High |
| Timing | Controls when escapes are realistic | Medium |
| Inventory / tools | Supports specific routes and plans | Medium |

### How to Use Voice Without Making Things Worse

Voice interaction is Militsioner's most distinctive feature, and also the one most likely to backfire for beginners. Speak clearly and keep responses short. If your environment is noisy, or you share a room, check the settings for input options before you rely on speech mid-escape.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Speak in short, clear sentences | Ramble or repeat yourself |
| Match the tone of the character you're addressing | Threaten the Giant Policeman early |
| Use conversation to stall and observe | Talk while sneaking through open ground |
| Test your mic before tense moments | Assume every NPC reacts the same way |

## Your First Hour: A Step-by-Step Plan

Your opening hour should be about information, not heroics. Community reports suggest that the players who escape fastest are the ones who spend their first run mapping the town instead of sprinting for the nearest exit.

| Step | Action | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Walk the perimeter of your starting area | Reveals exits, cover, and patrol sightlines |
| 2 | Test the voice system on a low-stakes character | Builds confidence before high-pressure talks |
| 3 | Note where the Giant Policeman can and cannot see | Defines your safe movement corridors |
| 4 | Identify one short-term objective | Gives your run direction without overcommitting |
| 5 | Attempt a small, low-risk action | Teaches you how the game punishes mistakes |
| 6 | Retreat and reassess if attention spikes | Prevents an early, avoidable capture |

Once you have that mental map, your second run will feel dramatically easier. You will know which alleys are dead ends, which conversations are worth having, and roughly how long you can stay in the open before the officer's attention becomes a problem.

## Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

Most early failures come from a small set of habits. Fix these and your escape rate climbs quickly.

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | The Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sprinting everywhere | Noise and motion draw the gaze | Walk; sprint only to break line of sight |
| Ignoring dialogue | Misses information and safe options | Talk to characters before acting |
| Fixating on one escape route | Routes close when attention rises | Always have a backup plan |
| Hoarding items you never use | Wastes time and inventory space | Carry only what a route needs |
| Panicking when spotted | Leads to running into worse positions | Break sight, then stop and reassess |
| Skipping the map entirely | Guarantees you get cornered | Explore before you commit |

A useful rule of thumb: if you feel rushed, you probably are. Militsioner rewards patience far more than speed, and a slow, deliberate approach usually beats a fast, noisy one.

## FAQ

**Do I need a microphone to play Militsioner?**
Voice input is the game's signature feature, so a working microphone gives you the fullest experience. If you cannot use one, check the input settings before starting a run so you are not caught out mid-conversation.

**How do I avoid the Giant Policeman's gaze?**
Stay behind solid cover, avoid long open streets, and move in short bursts rather than long sprints. Elevation and distance matter as much as walls do.

**Should I talk or run when I get noticed?**
Break line of sight first. Once you are hidden, a calm conversation often buys more time than another sprint, especially early in a run.

**Is there anything a Militsioner beginner guide can't teach me?**
Routes and dialogue outcomes shift with your attention level and choices, so a Militsioner beginner guide can only take you so far. The rest comes from experimenting, noting what worked, and refining your own escape plan run by run.
