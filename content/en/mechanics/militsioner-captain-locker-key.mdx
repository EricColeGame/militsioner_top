## Where to Find the Militsioner Captain Locker Key

Community reports on the Militsioner captain locker key are consistent on the broad strokes: the key is not sitting on a shelf, and it is not handed to you by a quest giver. Instead, the route runs through an **upper window**. According to a fan-maintained wiki writeup, the way in is to jump through the window on the upper level rather than walking through the front of the building.

That single detail reframes the whole approach. The Captain's shop is a place you will visit often to sell junk and buy supplies, so the temptation is to treat it as safe, familiar ground. The locker area is not. You are entering a space the game expects you to sneak into, and the timing of your climb matters as much as the climb itself.

Treat the exact window placement as player experience rather than gospel. Militsioner is a living project, and layout tweaks between builds can shift a jumpable ledge by a few feet. If a route that worked in a video does not line up in your session, scan the building's upper facade for anything that reads as climbable — crates, awnings, ledges, or a lower roof that feeds into the window.

## Stealth Tips for Reaching the Militsioner Captain Locker Key

The climb is only half the job. What gets players caught is the **pause** — that half-second where you stop to check a menu or line up a jump while standing in the open. Below are the habits that consistently show up in community reports from players who make the route work.

| Hazard | Tell | Counter |
|---|---|---|
| Long gaze from the Colossal Policeman | Shadow or framing shift over the district | Wait out a full patrol cycle before climbing |
| Ground-floor attention | NPCs turning toward the shop | Enter from above, never the front door |
| Getting greedy | Looting beyond the key | Grab the key, then leave |
| Sloppy exit | Reusing a watched path | Reverse your entry route |
| Overconfidence | Treating the shop as safe | Treat every visit as watched |

A few practical habits help more than any single trick:

- **Scout before you climb.** One full loop of the building tells you more than ten minutes of guessing.
- **Climb light.** Extra weight slows movement, and slow movement near a window is a beacon.
- **Never loot twice.** Take the key and the best one or two items, then go.
- **Bank your rubles.** Selling immediately converts risk into a permanent resource.
- **Rehearse the jump.** If a ledge is finicky, practice it once without entering.

The Xbox Wire announcement for the game frames the central tension well — you are meant to evade, or perhaps charm, an overwhelming authority figure. Either way, the game rewards players who plan a route instead of improvising under pressure.

## FAQ

**Is the Militsioner captain locker key required to escape?**
No. It is an economic shortcut rather than a hard requirement. Player experience suggests you can finish a run without it, but the rubles it generates make fines, supplies, and stealth gear far easier to manage.

**How do I get the Militsioner captain locker key if the window route fails?**
Re-scout the building's upper level. Community reports point to the upper window as the entry, but climbable props and layout details can shift between builds, so look for any ledge or roof that feeds into that opening.

**Does selling the locker contents to The Captain hurt my run?**
Not usually. Rubles are the point. Sell anything you have not used in a full in-game day and reinvest in tools that shorten your route to the train.

**When is the safest time to attempt the climb?**
Community gameplay coverage says the train runs from 6:00 am to 6:00 pm, so early-morning attempts give you a long day to recover from a mistake. Whatever hour you pick, watch a full patrol cycle first and climb during the gap.
