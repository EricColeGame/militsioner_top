## How to Give Flowers to the Giant Policeman

Getting the flowers is only half of the **Militsioner how to get flowers** question. The other half is delivery. The giant policeman is not a normal NPC you walk up to and chat with — he is a presence that looms over the entire map, so the approach matters as much as the item.

| Action | Recommended? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Approach at a normal pace | Yes | Reads as intentional rather than frantic |
| Have the flowers ready before you close the distance | Yes | Avoids fumbling in front of him |
| Sprint directly at him from a distance | No | Player experience suggests it looks like a provocation |
| Try to deliver mid-chase | No | Wait until the situation is calm |
| Give up after one attempt | No | The interaction is a moment, not a mission objective |

Think of it as a timing minigame built on mood. You want the officer in a neutral state, you want your inventory sorted, and you want a clean approach line. If the moment does not come, break off, do something ordinary for a while, and try again later — the bouquet will keep.

It also helps to know what you are not getting. There is no confirmed reward table for the gesture, and different players report different reactions. What is confirmed is that the interaction exists and that the developers clearly built it as a deliberate feature rather than an easter egg.

## FAQ

**Militsioner how to get flowers — what is the fastest method?**
Clear residential interiors first. The community wiki lists six known spawn points, mostly inside citizens' homes, so a loop through enterable houses beats wandering the streets. Check vases along the way for random drops.

**Do flowers respawn after you pick them up?**
Fixed spawns are the dependable source and may not come back once collected. Vase flowers are described as random, so a cleared building is still worth revisiting on a later run — player experience suggests the RNG can surprise you.

**Can you give flowers to anyone besides the giant policeman?**
The confirmed, headline interaction is the one with the colossal officer, which press coverage highlighted specifically. Other recipients are not documented, so treat any additional use as unconfirmed community speculation.

**Are flowers required to finish Militsioner?**
No. They are an optional pickup tied to a social interaction rather than a progression gate. That said, they are one of the few friendly gestures available in a game about suspicion, which makes them worth the detour if you enjoy the role-playing side of the immersive sim.
