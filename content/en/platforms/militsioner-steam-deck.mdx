## Militsioner Steam Deck Status: How to Read Valve's Compatibility Label

An "Unknown" rating is not a verdict. It simply means Valve's compatibility review team has not finished evaluating the build. Because Militsioner is still in development and scheduled for a 2027 release, that is entirely normal — most unreleased games carry the same placeholder.

| Steam Deck badge | What Valve means by it | Current Militsioner status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Verified** | Runs great out of the box, default controls, readable text, no manual tweaks needed | Not awarded |
| **Playable** | Runs, but may require manual settings or show minor issues | Not awarded |
| **Unknown** | Valve has not completed its review yet | This is where Militsioner sits |
| **Unsupported** | Will not run properly on the hardware | Not the case |

The practical takeaway: you should not assume the game is broken on Deck, and you should not assume it is optimized either. Treat the badge as "unconfirmed" and verify with the demo before you commit to a purchase.

You can track the official listing yourself on the [Militsioner Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1373530/Militsioner/), where the badge updates automatically once Valve finishes testing. Valve also explains the criteria behind each rating on its [Steam Deck compatibility review page](https://www.steamdeck.com/en/verified).

Two conclusions follow. First, because only the operating system and storage are listed, nobody can judge performance headroom from the spec sheet alone — including Valve. **Practical tip:** install the demo to a microSD card first. If performance is acceptable, you can move it to internal storage later for faster load times without having wasted a large internal install.

## How to Test Militsioner Steam Deck Performance Yourself

You do not need to wait for a Verified badge. The demo plus the Deck's built-in performance overlay gives you a solid read in about 30 minutes.

1. **Install the demo** from the store page — internal storage first if you have room, microSD otherwise.
2. **Force a Proton version** if the game does not launch. In the game's Properties menu, open Compatibility and try the latest Proton build or Proton Experimental.
3. **Start at low or medium presets.** Upscaling options such as FSR are your friend on a 1280×800 panel.
4. **Set a frame-rate cap.** A locked 30 or 40 FPS usually feels better than a fluctuating 60 on a handheld.
5. **Open the performance overlay** (levels 3 or 4) so you can watch frame times, not just average FPS.
6. **Play the noisiest scene you can find** — a crowded town square, a chase, or a scripted sequence with the Colossal Policeman on screen.

| What to log | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Average FPS | Your baseline smoothness |
| 1% lows / frame-time spikes | Stutter is more noticeable than a low average |
| Estimated battery life | Stealth games reward long sessions |
| Fan noise and chassis heat | Affects comfort in handheld mode |
| HUD and subtitle readability | Militsioner ships story-rich text in 14 languages |
| Suspend / resume behavior | Critical for pick-up-and-play sessions |

If the demo holds a stable frame rate in the busiest areas, the full game will most likely behave similarly at launch, barring major engine changes.

## Does a Surveillance Stealth Sim Fit a Handheld?

Genre fit is underrated in compatibility discussions. A game can run flawlessly and still be a poor handheld experience. Militsioner's design leans in a mostly favorable direction.

| Design element | Handheld impact |
| --- | --- |
| First-person stealth and parkour | Sticks plus gyro aiming work well |
| Open-ended, explorable town | Suspend and resume friendly |
| Multiple escape routes and replay value | Rewards short, repeat sessions |
| Dystopian, atmospheric horror tone | Headphones strongly recommended |
| Story-rich dialogue with 14 subtitle languages | Small-screen text is the main risk |
| Mood management and real-time tactics | Pause-heavy pacing suits portable play |

The one genuine concern is text density. Militsioner's subtitle list covers English, Russian, French, Italian, German, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Ukrainian — with full English voice audio. On a 7-inch panel, dialogue-heavy scenes and menu text can become a strain, though the Deck's zoom function helps.

Player experience with similar first-person immersive sims suggests that gyro-assisted aiming and a 40 Hz refresh cap strike the best balance between responsiveness and battery life.
