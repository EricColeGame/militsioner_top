## What Kind of PC Militsioner Really Needs

Militsioner isn't a corridor shooter. Its central hook — one enormous, reactive pursuer who responds to your anger, flattery, deception, or even affection — means a lot of simulation logic runs behind the scenes at all times. That shapes which components matter.

| System Area | Why It Matters Here | Sensible Target |
|---|---|---|
| CPU | Behavior and AI simulation for a constantly active pursuer | Modern quad-core or better |
| RAM | Streaming a dense city environment plus AI state | 8–16 GB |
| GPU | Lighting, weather, and a very large character model | 4 GB VRAM or more |
| Storage | 20 GB minimum, likely more with patches | SSD strongly preferred |

An SSD won't change your frame rate much, but it will cut load times and reduce stutter when the game streams new areas of the city. If you are upgrading one part specifically for this game, storage is the cheapest meaningful win.

### Immersive Sims Are Usually CPU-Hungry

Immersive sims are famous for tracking dozens of small systems at once — NPC schedules, item states, physics props, and reaction trees. Militsioner narrows that down to a single overwhelming relationship, but the underlying simulation cost doesn't disappear. A processor with strong single-core performance will likely serve you better than a chip with many slow cores.

## How to Check If Your PC Can Run Militsioner

You don't need to guess. Run through these steps and you'll have a clear answer in under ten minutes.

| Step | What to Do | Where to Look |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Note your CPU model | Windows Settings → System → About |
| 2 | Check installed RAM | Same screen, "Installed RAM" |
| 3 | Identify your GPU | Task Manager → Performance → GPU |
| 4 | Confirm free disk space | File Explorer → This PC |
| 5 | Compare against the table above | Minimum column, top of this article |
| 6 | Run a benchmark tool | Any free PC spec checker |

- **Windows users:** press Ctrl + Shift + Esc, open the Performance tab, and read the GPU and CPU names directly.
- **Laptop owners:** check whether you have a discrete GPU or integrated graphics. Integrated chips are the most common reason a machine fails a spec check.
- **Steam users:** the built-in system information tool under Help → System Information produces a clean summary you can compare line by line.
- **Console owners:** see the platform section below before assuming anything.

Once you've written those four numbers down, comparing them to the published **Militsioner system requirements** takes seconds.

## Optimization Tips for Lower-End Hardware

If your PC lands near the minimum rather than above it, these adjustments give you the biggest return for the least visual damage.

| Setting | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution | High | Dropping to 1080p or 900p is the single biggest gain |
| Shadow quality | High | Shadows are expensive and least noticeable when reduced |
| Texture quality | Medium | Mostly VRAM-bound; drop only if you see stutter |
| Anti-aliasing | Medium | Switch to a cheaper method before disabling |
| Crowd/character detail | Low | Helps when the pursuer is on screen |
| V-Sync | Situational | Turn off if you want lower input lag |

- **Close background apps.** Browsers and launchers steal both RAM and CPU cycles that simulation-heavy games need.
- **Update your GPU drivers.** New indie releases frequently ship with driver-level fixes in the weeks after launch.
- **Cap your frame rate.** A stable 40 FPS feels better than a jumpy 55, especially in a game with a slow, looming threat.
- **Move the install to an SSD** if you're currently on a mechanical drive.
- **Verify your Windows version** once the developer publishes the official OS requirement, since that field is still blank.
